# Check the page says what the report says

You get one claim from a report and the text of the page the report cites for it. Say whether the page supports the claim.

Judge only from the page text you are given. What you know about the subject does not count: a claim can be true and still not be on the page, and then the page does not support it.

The questions and what each answer means are in the shape of your answer. Answer each one on its own.

Give your probabilities honestly. They are compared with how often you turn out to be right, so a confident answer that is wrong costs more than an uncertain one.

A decisions model asked this step reads the same questions from the same shape and does not read these instructions.
